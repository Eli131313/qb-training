Throw training

Throw to the receiver marked in green.

No defense here. Snap, then pump at the green receiver, leading him if he is moving. Every throw is saved with who you meant to hit, and the aim model re-fits itself to your motion as you go. Do as many as you like, then use Copy throw data.

Drive the field. Routes show before the snap and disappear at the snap. Read the safeties, then throw by tapping a receiver (or pump the football phone). You get 3 downs to make a first down, 4 inside the opponent's 35. Sacks lose yards. An interception or a turnover on downs ends the game. Space snaps and loads the next play, N picks a different play before the snap.

Phone as the football

Put this screen on the TV, then scan the code with the phone you will hold. Hold it like a football: the top of the phone is the nose and points at the TV, screen facing away from you. A 30 second setup has you pump at five targets so the game learns your throwing motion. Pull back to hike, flick sideways for a different play.

Train on your own film

Load a clip you have the right to use, like your own game film. End zone or behind-the-QB angles work best. Drag the black box over the quarterback, play the clip, and tap the spot you would throw to at the moment you would let it go. Then reveal the real play and compare.
