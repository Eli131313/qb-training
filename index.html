Throw training

Throw to the receiver marked in green.

No defense here. Snap, then pump at the green receiver, leading him if he is moving. Every throw is saved with who you meant to hit, and the aim model re-fits itself to your motion as you go. Do as many as you like, then use Copy throw data.

Routes are drawn before the snap and disappear when the ball is snapped. Read the safeties, then throw by tapping a receiver on the field. Space snaps and loads the next play. You are graded on who you picked and when the ball came out.

Phone as the football

Put this screen on the TV, then scan the code with the phone you will hold. Hold it like a football: the bottom of the phone is the nose. A 30 second setup has you pump at five targets so the game learns your throwing motion.

Train on your own film

Load a clip you have the right to use, like your own game film. End zone or behind-the-QB angles work best. Drag the black box over the quarterback, play the clip, and tap the spot you would throw to at the moment you would let it go. Then reveal the real play and compare.

Hold it like a football in your throwing hand. The bottom of the phone is the nose of the ball and points at the TV. The top of the phone sits back by your ear. Grip it tight or strap it to a real ball.
